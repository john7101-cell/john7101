# VOLVER A MÍ — Prompts para Flow (Veo)

Ajustes en Flow: **9:16 vertical**, clips de **8 s**, modelo Veo (Quality si alcanzan los créditos).
Pide **sin texto ni subtítulos** en la imagen (los textos los pongo yo en la edición) y solo sonido ambiente.

## Personaje (pégalo al inicio de cada prompt o créalo como "ingrediente")

> A Colombian woman in her mid-30s, warm olive skin, shoulder-length wavy dark brown hair, natural makeup, small gold earrings, elegant and confident. Wearing a cream tailored blazer over a soft blush silk blouse.

Estilo común (al final de cada prompt):

> Cinematic, shallow depth of field, soft natural light, warm rose and plum color grade, subtle film grain, vertical 9:16. No text, no subtitles, no logos. Ambient sound only.

---

## Historia 1 — El gancho (2 clips)

**1A · Líder en la oficina**
> [Personaje] walks confidently through a modern glass office in Medellín, morning light, colleagues greet her, she stops at a meeting table and points at a laptop screen while the team nods. Smooth tracking shot following her from the front. [Estilo]

**1B · Del poder a la espera**
> [Personaje] alone in the same office at dusk, everyone gone, she sits at her desk, takes her phone out, looks at the screen and her confident expression softens into doubt. Slow push-in to her face lit by the phone. [Estilo]

## Historia 2 — «Pero… cuando te enamoras» (1 clip)

**2A · Esperando el mensaje**
> [Personaje] at night on a sofa in a dim apartment, wearing a soft sweater, holding her phone, she unlocks it, checks the chat, nothing, locks it, sighs, unlocks it again. Close-up on her hands and the glowing phone, then her face. City bokeh lights through the window. [Estilo]

## Historia 3 — El espejo emocional (2 clips)

**3A · Ilusión rápida**
> [Personaje] smiling while reading a message on her phone in a café, she hugs the phone to her chest, dreamy, then starts typing a long reply. Handheld, warm afternoon light. [Estilo]

**3B · No poder soltar**
> [Personaje] sitting on the edge of her bed at night, phone face down beside her, she picks it up, reads, her eyes fill with tears, she puts it down but keeps her hand on it, unable to let go. Static medium shot, then slow push-in. [Estilo]

## Historia 4 — La pregunta que transforma (2 clips)

**4A · Frente al espejo**
> [Personaje] in front of a bathroom mirror, soft side light, she looks at her reflection with sadness, touches the edge of the sink, takes a deep breath. Camera over her shoulder showing her reflection. [Estilo]

**4B · De la tristeza a la reflexión**
> Close-up of [Personaje]'s reflection in the mirror, her expression slowly shifts from sadness to calm reflection, she wipes a tear and holds her own gaze with quiet determination. Very slow push-in. [Estilo]

## Historia 5 — La propuesta (3 clips)

**5A · Llegando al encuentro**
> [Personaje] arriving at a rustic boutique hotel among green Andean mountains in Santa Elena, Antioquia, wooden architecture, flowers, she walks in with a soft hopeful smile. Wide shot then medium. Golden afternoon light. [Estilo]

**5B · Círculo de mujeres**
> A small circle of 7 diverse Latina women of different ages sitting on cushions in a cozy wooden lounge with plants and large windows to the mountains, one speaks while the others listen with empathy, [Personaje] among them nodding. Slow orbiting camera. [Estilo]

**5C · Compartir y reflexionar**
> Close-ups inside the women's circle: hands writing in a journal, a woman laughing gently, two women holding hands, [Personaje] smiling with tears of relief. Soft cuts, warm light. [Estilo]

## Historia 6 — La cita contigo (2 clips)

**6A · Una cita contigo**
> [Personaje] writes "5 de diciembre" in a paper planner with a pen and circles it with a heart, close-up of her hand, then she smiles and closes the planner. Morning light on a wooden table with coffee. [Estilo — aquí sí puede haber texto escrito a mano en la agenda]

**6B · The Ranch Hotel Boutique**
> Aerial drone shot descending over green mountains and pine forest in Santa Elena, Antioquia, revealing a charming rustic boutique hotel with wooden cabins and gardens, golden afternoon light, mist in the valleys. [Estilo]

## Historia 7 — Volver a ti (3 clips)

**7A · No se trata de dejar de amar**
> [Personaje] and a man share a tender moment on a balcony, she laughs, but this time she is relaxed and present, confident, not anxious, she puts the phone away. Medium shot, warm sunset. [Estilo]

**7B · Amar sin perderte**
> [Personaje] alone outdoors in a green mountain meadow at golden hour, eyes closed, hand on her heart, wind in her hair, a slow peaceful smile, she opens her eyes and looks at the camera with serenity. Slow push-in. [Estilo]

**7C · Cierre**
> [Personaje] walks towards the camera on a garden path of the boutique hotel at golden hour, confident and free, she stops, smiles directly at the camera and gives a small inviting nod. Leave empty space in the lower third of the frame. [Estilo]
